02 / The operator

People first.
Systems follow.

I started at Sainsbury's, running a 126-person team across 10 sites. Not managing from above, in the middle of it. Safety, training, performance, culture. The full weight of large-scale people operations.

Then came Web3. Not as a speculator but as a builder. Years deep in the ecosystem: exchanges, NFT projects, early-stage startups. I made money, lost money, and learned to build infrastructure that outlasts a cycle. When regulation arrived, I moved toward it, not away.

Today I sit at the intersection most operators never reach: large-team experience, startup velocity, and regulated-industry compliance. I've worked with boards, product teams, engineering squads, and regulators. I treat everyone the same. The work earns the room.
